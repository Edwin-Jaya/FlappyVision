01 / The experience

A simple gesture.
A playful interaction.

WisdomPaws brings together real-time perception and generative AI in a small, playful desktop experience.

⌁

See your gesture

Real-time hand tracking detects an open palm through your camera using MediaPipe.

✧

Generate a response

An LLM turns the interaction into a contextual response, bringing generative AI into the experience.

♡

Meet your virtual pet

A Pygame-powered companion presents the interaction through a character and on-screen feedback.

02 / Under the hood

From camera to
character.

A modular pipeline connects physical input, gesture interpretation, language generation, and visual feedback.

STEP 01

Camera input

OpenCV captures live video from the webcam.

STEP 02

Hand tracking

MediaPipe detects hand landmarks in the frame.

STEP 03

Gesture event

An open palm triggers an interaction.

STEP 04

LLM response

The language model generates contextual wisdom.

STEP 05

Pet feedback

Pygame displays the response and animation.

03 / Built with

Where perception meets generation.

WisdomPaws explores how computer vision and language models can work together in an interactive application. The project is organized around a small Python codebase, separating application flow, interaction control, rendering, and LLM integration.
